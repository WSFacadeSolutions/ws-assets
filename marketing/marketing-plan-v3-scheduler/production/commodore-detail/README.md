# The Old Commodore Hotel — finish details

A second, materially distinct Commodore carousel for Marketing Plan v3. This
sequence stays close to the finishes and does not repeat the spatial post's
views. It carries no logo, overlay, typography or template treatment.

## Sequence

1. Venetian plaster and handrail — the hero material detail.
2. Handrail, wall finish and stair lighting — controlled junctions.
3. Concrete-finish wall and timber junction — surface depth at close range.
4. Wet-area wall and joinery — finish continuity under a different use case.

## Caption

The Old Commodore Hotel, McMahons Point — close view.

The character of a finish is decided at the edges: where plaster meets timber,
where a handrail tracks a wall, where light moves across texture and where one
substrate becomes another.

The Commodore's interior package included 120 square metres of Venetian plaster
and 111 square metres of concrete finish. Those figures describe the scale; the
close views show the control required to make that scale feel consistent.

Materials with depth. Junctions with restraint. One finish language carried
through the building.

Venetian plaster · Concrete finishes · Detailed junctions
