# The Old Commodore Hotel — spatial sequence

A clean, photography-led carousel for Marketing Plan v3. It uses four distinct
interior views from the project website archive and carries no logo, overlay,
typography or template treatment.

## Sequence

1. Long bar and dining-room view — spatial establishing image.
2. Stair and circulation junction — transition and material continuity.
3. Bar threshold — joinery, ceiling and circulation working together.
4. Dining room — the completed hospitality setting at full scale.

## Caption

The Old Commodore Hotel, McMahons Point.

Hospitality finishes have to do more than read well in a photograph. They need
to hold together across long sightlines, busy circulation and constant contact.

At The Commodore, the interior package moved between Venetian plaster walls,
concrete-finish structural columns and the transitions around the bar, stair and
dining spaces. The challenge was continuity: different substrates and different
rooms, but one resolved interior.

Managed as a complete finishing package, the result feels consistent from the
first view through to the details.

Interior finishing · Venetian plaster · Concrete finishes
