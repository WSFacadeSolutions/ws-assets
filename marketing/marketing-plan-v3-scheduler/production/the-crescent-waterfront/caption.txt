The Crescent, Vaucluse, NSW

Seen from the water's edge, the house becomes part of a much larger composition.

Rendered planes, dark screens and deep overhangs sit between sandstone and mature planting. WS delivered render, decorative finishes, paint and caulking across the home; from this distance, their role is clear — give the architecture definition without separating it from the landscape.
