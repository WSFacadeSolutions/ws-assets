## Peakhurst interior

A quieter view of a complete finishing package.

## Drummoyne threshold

The facade does not stop at the front elevation.

## The Crescent outdoor room

The Crescent, Vaucluse, NSW

An outdoor room shaped by one continuous curve.

The architecture opens to the landscape, holds deep shade and guides movement around the
built-in seating. Our work had to support that composition without competing with it.

Render, decorative finishes, paint and caulking were brought together across the space,
leaving stone, planting and shadow to lead the experience.
