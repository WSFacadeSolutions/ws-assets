The best finishing packages are not managed as a collection of isolated trades.

At The Old Commodore Hotel in McMahons Point, the interior scope moved between Venetian plaster walls, concrete-finish structural columns and the junctions around the bar, stair and dining spaces.

Each finish had its own application requirements. The harder problem was making them read as one interior across long sightlines, busy circulation and constant contact.

That is why coordination matters as much as application. Sequencing, substrate readiness, protection and handover all affect the final surface — even when they are no longer visible.

WS delivered the interior and exterior finishing package as one managed scope.
