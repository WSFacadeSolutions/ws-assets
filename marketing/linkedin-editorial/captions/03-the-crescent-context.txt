Close-up finish photographs are useful, but distance tells a different truth.

Seen from the water's edge, The Crescent in Vaucluse becomes a composition of rendered planes, dark screens, deep overhangs, sandstone and mature planting. The facade has to give the architecture definition without separating the home from its setting.

WS delivered render, decorative finishes, paint and caulking across the project. Those individual scopes disappear in the completed view — which is exactly the point. A coordinated finishing package should support the architecture, not ask to be noticed as a collection of trades.
