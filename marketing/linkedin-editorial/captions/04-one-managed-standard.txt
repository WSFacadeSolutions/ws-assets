Consistency on site does not come from asking every person to work in exactly the same way. It comes from making the required standard clear, checking the conditions early and keeping decisions close to the work.

At WS, Heron de Freitas Santiago and Thomas Wilhelm lead from both sides of that responsibility: engineering, project management and business operations alongside rendering, site logistics and workforce performance.

The objective is straightforward — different specialists, one managed standard.

That means issues are raised before they become defects, resources are treated with care, and the finished surface is judged as part of the whole project.

Many trades. One contract. One shared responsibility for the result.
