# `WS` Highlight — story-native sequence

## Purpose

Give a new profile visitor a fast, credible answer to four questions: who WS is,
what WS delivers, how WS manages quality and how to make contact. This is not a
vertical crop of the 72-second capability film.

## Recommended sequence

| Story | Role | Primary copy | Visual direction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | Open with evidence | `WS Facade Solutions` / `Finishing packages. One managed standard.` | Full-screen completed-project photograph; company name restrained, no logo panel. |
| 02 | Put people behind the standard | `Led by Heron and Thomas.` | Rooftop team photograph with names and roles only. |
| 03 | Service map, part one | `Rendering` · `Decorative Finishes` · `Painting` · `Substrate Installation` | One strong project image plus four large service markers. |
| 04 | Service map, part two | `Caulking and Sealant Application` · `Remedial Services & Concrete Repair` · `Asset Conservation and Maintenance` · `Labour Hire` | A second, visually distinct project image plus four large service markers. No Labour Hire photograph is implied. |
| 05 | State the operating principle | `Precision through care.` / `People. Client resources. Relationships.` | Quiet editorial composition with one human or material detail. |
| 06 | Show verified system capability | `Systems we are certified to apply.` | The current applicator marks, normalised for legibility and arranged for a phone screen. |
| 07 | Close the quality argument | `Compliance focus in every layer.` / `Strict compliance. On-site presence. Precise execution.` | Restrained three-part composition; no small explanatory prose. |
| 08 | Make the next action obvious | `WS Facade Solutions Pty Ltd` / ABN, NSW contractor licence, email, phone and website | Clean credentials page with the official lockup. |

## Motion and sound

- 9:16, 1080 × 1920, with every critical element inside Story interface-safe zones.
- Each Story must work as an independent frame; movement is restrained and never
  travels downwards against the viewer's tap/scroll behaviour.
- Use one new, original soundtrack authored for this sequence and embedded into
  the exported MP4 segments. Do not depend on Instagram's music sticker.
- The soundtrack may continue conceptually across the eight segments, but each
  exported segment must begin and end cleanly because viewers may enter the
  Highlight part-way through.
- Avoid paragraphs, tiny desktop copy and the 16:9 film's full scene density.

## Publishing order

1. Confirm Story Archive is enabled.
2. Publish the eight Stories in order.
3. Create or update the `WS` Highlight and select all eight Stories.
4. Set `01-ws.png` as the cover or use its uploaded cover Story if the account's
   app build does not offer direct gallery selection.
5. Review the live circular crop and the first/last frames on a second device.

The feed Reel remains a separate decision. A Reel should only be produced later
if it has its own discovery purpose; the `WS` Highlight already covers profile
orientation and capability proof.
