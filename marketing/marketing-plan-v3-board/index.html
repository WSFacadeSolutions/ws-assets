Marketing Plan v3

Let the work lead.

A practical editorial reset for Instagram and LinkedIn, built from real WS projects and designed for the capacity available now.

Reference, not replication

Less campaign on the image. More evidence in the frame.

Blue Label is useful as a reference for confidence in photography and visual variety. The opportunity for WS is not to borrow their identity. It is to give our own projects, details and point of view enough space to be seen.

01Keep the system backstage: selection, crop, fact checking and caption structure.
02Remove routine logos, blurred panels and repeated title screens.
03Use designed editorial covers only when the idea earns the treatment.
