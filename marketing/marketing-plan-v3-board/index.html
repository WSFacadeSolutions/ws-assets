Archive finding · not scheduled

Use the space we genuinely have.

The archive does not support a broad interiors category yet. It does support one convincing Peakhurst editorial feature and two accurate indoor–outdoor readings. These can diversify the feed without pretending WS has photographed work it has not.

Strongest opportunityInterior editorial

Peakhurst · surface as atmosphere

A proper editorial carousel: room first, material detail second, then one restrained paragraph about how the decorative finish changes the space. This is strong enough for the feed after scope and credits are confirmed.

Accurate framingCovered outdoor room

The Crescent · room without a door

Use it as architecture and atmosphere, not as an interior. The curved seating, deep shade and garden edge give us a distinct spatial post beyond another facade.

Secondary optionIndoor–outdoor threshold

Drummoyne · the edge of the room

A quieter supporting post about the transition between the protected interior and the external deck. Useful for feed rhythm once the exact WS scope is confirmed.

Recommendation: produce Peakhurst first. Keep The Crescent and Drummoyne in the next-production bank. Do not label the broader WS archive as an interiors portfolio until future shoots deliberately capture more completed rooms.

Field craft can become premium content.

Serginho's archive contains strong gestures and process, but not every project is necessarily WS work. The right treatment is a calm, hard-cut edit with fresh licensed or original sound — no CapCut marks, stickers, third-party Instagram music or generic report footage.

The finish is in the pressure

One precise wall-finishing action, tightly reframed. Let hand, tool and material carry the film; keep the English caption minimal.

Review team source reel

Hands behind the finish

Three to five precise gestures from approved WS sites. Different projects may be combined only when the caption clearly presents them as a craft study, not one job.
