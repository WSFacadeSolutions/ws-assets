Foundation

Brand DNA

The personality of WS is built around three primary attributes that guide every creative decision — from logo application to tone of voice.

Cuidadosa Inovadora Dinâmica Elegante Confiável Clean Inteligente Coerente Promissora Transparente Resolutiva

A marca WS deve manter equilíbrio entre inovação, modernidade e sofisticação. Abordagem minimalista — não simplista. Esse meio-termo é essencial para uma boa aplicação.

Guidelines

Logo Usage Rules

Consistency builds recognition. Never alter any aspect of the logo — colours, proportions, rotation, or typography.

01
Do not alter the logo colour outside the approved palette
02
Do not rotate the logo
03
Do not distort or stretch proportions
04
Do not apply on low-contrast backgrounds
05
Do not use non-approved background colours
06
No gradients or special effects on the logo
07
No drop shadows
08
Do not change the logo typeface
SOCIAL MEDIA — AVATAR SPEC

Use the symbol (icon only) for all social avatars. Square format: 1000 × 1000 px. Symbol at 477px height, 700px width, centred.
