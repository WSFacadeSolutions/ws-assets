Safe Zone / Protected Area

Maintain clear space equal to the height of the WS brick icon on all sides. No text, imagery, or competing marks within this zone. Minimum sizes: horizontal print 55mm · screen 180px — symbol print 12mm · screen 35px.

Guidelines
Logo Usage Rules

Consistency builds recognition. Never alter any aspect of the logo — colour, proportions, rotation, or typography.

01
Do not alter the logo colour outside the approved palette
02
Do not rotate the logo at any angle
03
Do not distort or stretch proportions
04
Do not apply on low-contrast or unapproved backgrounds
05
Do not use non-approved background colours
06
No gradients or special effects applied to the logo
07
No drop shadows or outer glow
08
Do not change or replace the logo typeface
Social Media Avatar Spec

Use the WS Square mark for all social avatars. Canvas: 1000 × 1000 px. Mark at 477px height × 700px width, centred on canvas.
