# WS Field Stories — Pack 01

Three short, footage-led Stories designed as documentary site notes rather than
campaign templates. Each opens on usable footage, uses hard cuts and restrained
English lower thirds, carries no logo card, and removes all inherited source
audio.

## Compositions

- **Serginho** — 13.1 seconds. Four approved 720 × 1280 craft excerpts covering
  team-scale work, preparation, curved-wall application and the shared finish.
  No project or location claim is made.
- **Floor protection** — 8.9 seconds. Verified WS614 footage from Dover Heights,
  NSW: protected surface, perimeter detail and field execution.
- **High-build junction** — 9 seconds. Verified WS614 footage from Dover Heights,
  NSW: scaffold context, façade junction and the controlled build-up detail.

The three cues are original and intentionally different: a brighter brushed
rhythm for Serginho, a softer preparation pulse for floor protection, and a
slower structural bed for the high-build junction.

## Editorial use

The Dover Heights pieces can run as Stories and be saved under **Work**. The
Serginho cut is better used as a transient craft Story or as a supporting Reel,
not as the permanent Team-cover image: the moving process is the proof point and
no unverified Australian location is implied.

Full MP4 rendering and publication remain operator actions in WS Film after the
check stills are approved.
