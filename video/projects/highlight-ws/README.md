# Instagram Highlight — WS

This project turns the approved capability argument into eight Story-native,
six-second chapters. The 48-second master is a review and archive artefact; the
publishable deliverables are the eight files under `segments/`.

The sequence is designed to work without sound. Its original embedded score is
continuous across the master and is divided at exact chapter boundaries when
the Story files are produced.

Publishing order:

1. Schedule `01` through `08` in order under the `HIGHLIGHT · WS` campaign.
2. After publication, add all eight archived Stories to the `WS` Highlight.
3. Apply `01-ws.png` from the Instagram Highlights cover set.
4. Confirm the circular crop and sequence order in the Instagram app.

The Instagram API cannot add archived Stories to a profile Highlight, so step 2
is intentionally a short manual action for Guilherme.
